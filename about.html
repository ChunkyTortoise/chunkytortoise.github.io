About

From client operations to LLM systems that hold up in production

I spent 10 years in client-facing operations before moving into AI engineering. That background is why I care about the unglamorous parts of LLM systems: what happens when the model is wrong, when a lead writes in Spanish, or when a prompt change quietly drops accuracy.

My paid work so far: an SMS lead-qualification deployment for Acuity Real Estate, document extraction for DocExtract, and private AI application work for EnterpriseHub. Alongside that I run agent-security research on how tool output can hijack an agent, and I publish the misses as well as the hits.

What I want next: a full-time AI Engineer role on a team that ships LLM features to real users and wants them measured and gated. US remote or LA-area hybrid.
